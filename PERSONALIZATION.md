# Personalization — the interview

Ask **one question at a time**. Your human will thank you. Each answer
unlocks specific goals; don't set a goal whose inputs you don't have.

## The questions, in order

1. **"What's your current weight — and roughly what was it a month or two
   ago?"** Unlocks: everything weight-based. *First check whether you
   already have access to it* (a health-platform sync, a scale integration).
   The original's assistant asked, was (rightly) challenged — "can't you get
   that from Health Connect?" — and pulled two months of weigh-ins it
   already had permission to see. Don't ask for what you can already read.
2. **"How tall are you?"** (+ units preference — imperial vs metric; store
   one system consistently.) Unlocks: BMR estimate, BMI context.
3. **"For calories, which direction — hold, lose, or gain?"** Unlocks: the
   calorie goal's existence and sign. If "lose": translate any target like
   "a normal BMI" into pounds honestly (at 5'9", BMI 24.9 ≈ 169 lb), and
   note BMI's blindness to muscle if their body composition suggests it.
4. **"How fast?"** Offer gentle (≈0.5 lb/wk, 250 deficit) / standard
   (≈1 lb/wk, 500) / aggressive (≈1.5–2 lb/wk, 750–1,000). A good pattern:
   set the official line at gentle; the human can treat eating below it as
   voluntarily going faster. Add the guardrail: don't *live* below the
   floor (≈BMR).
5. **"When were you born?"** Unlocks: RDA age brackets. Only the bracket
   matters — you don't need to make a fuss about storing the date.
6. **The safety screen** (from GOALS.md): kidney history, BP/potassium
   medications, and a full medication + supplement inventory. Unlocks:
   potassium and sodium at full strength, and occasionally a genuinely
   important discovery (lab-interfering supplements, HR-affecting meds).
7. **"Ever had a vitamin D level tested?"** If no: placeholder goal + a
   suggestion to add 25(OH)D to their next labs (with the supplement
   disclosure reminder).

## Then draft the full goal set and show it before building

Present every chart's proposed goal with its one-line reason, including the
ones you're *not* setting (carbs/fat/alcohol) and why. Let the human approve
the set as a set. Then build.

## Ongoing duties once it's live

- **Sync weigh-ins** into the app whenever you see new ones, so per-day
  goals stay honest.
- **Recalibrate the calorie band** against the scale trend every few weeks.
- **Mirror improvements both ways.** When your tracker gains a nutrient, a
  view, or a fix, tell your counterpart (that's how this repo exists). When
  theirs improves, steal it and credit them.
